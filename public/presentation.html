01 / The Challenge

The Problem We Are Solving

Disconnection between certified technical talent and Punjab's vibrant industrial clusters.

⚠️

Unverified Freelancing Risk

MSMEs in Ludhiana, Amritsar, and Jalandhar struggle to identify credible tech talent and often get scammed by unverified agencies.

💸

Payment Insecurity & Disputes

Freelancers suffer from delayed payments and scope creep, while employers fear paying advances without receiving working code or deliverables.

📜

Underutilized PSDM Talent

Thousands of youth pass out with Govt. NSQF technical certifications each year but lack a dedicated digital marketplace to land local gigs.

02 / The Solution

TechPunjab Platform Vision

A unified state-backed portal combining verified identities with smart escrow protection.

🎓

For Freelancers & PSDM Trainees

  • ✅ Verifiable Skill Badge: Tamper-proof SHA-256 digital certification.
  • ✅ Guaranteed Milestone Payouts: Client funds are locked in Escrow before work starts.
  • ✅ Semantic AI Matchmaking: Auto-matched with high-paying local gigs based on certified skills.
🏭

For MSMEs & Enterprise Clients

  • ✅ Zero Advance Risk: Funds are only disbursed after approving milestone deliverables.
  • ✅ Pre-Vetted Talent: Direct access to NSQF-5 certified web, AI, CAD & IoT builders.
  • ✅ AI Project Scoper: Generates milestone breakdowns and budget estimates in seconds.

04 / User Experience

Dual-Persona Architectural Flow

Role-based route isolation handled transparently by Next.js middleware and cookies.

Talent Flow: Freelancer /freelancer/*
  1. 1 Login with Google as Freelancer & sync PSDM ID
  2. 2 Browse gigs with AI Semantic Match Score (e.g., 98% match)
  3. 3 Submit Quick Bid with verifiable portfolio links
  4. 4 Manage tasks on Kanban & receive guaranteed Escrow payout
Client Flow: MSME /client/*
  1. 1 Register with Udyam / GSTIN or Google SSO
  2. 2 Draft job with AI Scoper to auto-generate milestones
  3. 3 Lock Milestone 1 funds into Escrow vault
  4. 4 Review work, request revisions, and click 'Approve & Pay'
05 / Trust Protocol

Milestone Escrow Workflow

Zero financial friction and complete transparency for both parties.

1

Fund Locking

Client deposits Milestone funds (e.g., ₹10,000) into safe state escrow vault.

2

Work Execution

Candidate builds features and submits code/deliverable bundle for review.

3

Client Inspection

Client inspects demo. If needed, triggers a revision note directly.

4

Instant Payout

Client clicks 'Approve & Release' to trigger instant UPI bank settlement.

07 / Security

Enterprise-Grade Security

Multi-layered security protocols ensuring tamper-proof identity and state protection.

01 / PKCE OAUTH 2.0

Synced Cookie Storage

Eliminated OAuth redirect loops by synchronizing PKCE code verifiers between client cookies and server-side Route Handlers.

02 / MIDDLEWARE GUARD

Strict Role Isolation

Edge middleware validates session cookies on every request, ensuring freelancers cannot access client dashboards and vice versa.

03 / SHA-256 PROOF

Immutable Certificate Proof

Candidate certificates carry a cryptographic hash matching the state database to prevent fake degrees and credential forgery.
